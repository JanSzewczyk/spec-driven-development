---
title: /sdd:retro
description: Turn review lessons into constitution rules.
---

Optional, after [`/sdd:review`](/spec-driven-development/commands/review/). Reads `review.md` (blockers, violated rules, accepted items, drift) and proposes **WHAT NOT TO DO** entries for the constitution: only repeatable mistakes, only ones §5 does not already cover.

Each entry needs a `**Why:**` that comes from the review or from you; it asks instead of inventing one. Writes only constitution §5.
